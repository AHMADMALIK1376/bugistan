# Change Log

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-20

First release.

- Bugs spawn from the real errors and warnings in your active file, and respawn while the problem is still there.
- 38 hand-drawn creatures across 8 environments — mud, grass, night, desert, ocean, space, candy and cyber — all drawn on canvas, no image files.
- Slipper cursor: bugs panic and flee from it. Click to squash, +10 for an error, +5 for a warning, with a combo multiplier for quick kills.
- Squish sounds synthesised live with the Web Audio API — no audio files — and the pitch rises with your combo.
- Score kept in the status bar and saved between sessions.
- Settings for bug speed (including `pakistani`), maximum bugs on screen and sound.
